Today the **decdn** public testnet is live on Arbitrum Sepolia, and the code is open source. You can download something real from the network right now with one command, and you don't need a wallet to do it.

## The problem we kept hitting

Big files are awkward to move. Model weights, datasets and install images run to tens of gigabytes, and most of them come from one origin over one connection. When that origin throttles you, rate-limits you, or has a bad afternoon, you wait.

## What decdn does

Independent nodes cache content and serve it, and every file is addressed by its BLAKE3 hash. The client checks each piece against that hash the moment it arrives. A piece that fails is thrown out and fetched again from another node, so you end up with exactly the bytes the publisher named, without trusting any machine in between. Large files are pulled from several nodes at once.

Delivery is paid per megabyte, in USDC, as the bytes arrive. The target rate is $0.01/GB, 4 to 20 times cheaper than major traditional CDNs. It is a target, not a protocol-enforced price: nodes set their own rates, and every client can cap what it is willing to pay.

## The part we're proudest of: no wallet

The usual first step into a network that settles on-chain is "set up a wallet." Here it isn't. On the testnet a sponsor pays for your transfer. The installer opens one captcha in your browser, the sponsor grants a capped allowance for the download, and that's the whole setup. No seed phrase, no token, no sign-up.

## Try it

The homepage has a [catalogue](/#try) of things we seeded on the testnet: open model weights (Mistral 7B Instruct, Qwen2.5 7B Instruct, Whisper large-v3 and Stable Diffusion XL), the LibriSpeech speech corpus, Ubuntu and Debian install images, and two Blender open movies in 4K. Pick one and it shows you the exact line to paste.

For Mistral 7B Instruct, on macOS or Linux, in a terminal:

```text
curl -fsSL https://up.decdn.org/decdn.sh | sh -s -- pull b3:9194000d7b356650e6924a7746ec4afb0b705838b65913c0e46cba6b15af69e5 --namespace 1
```

On Windows, in PowerShell:

```text
& ([scriptblock]::Create((irm https://up.decdn.org/decdn.ps1))) pull b3:9194000d7b356650e6924a7746ec4afb0b705838b65913c0e46cba6b15af69e5 --namespace 1
```

The line installs decdn, runs the captcha, then downloads into the current directory and verifies every byte on the way in. If it gets interrupted, run the same line again and it resumes.

While it runs, you can watch the network work at [stats.decdn.org](https://stats.decdn.org): settlements and the node fleet, read straight off Arbitrum Sepolia. The whole thing is open source at [github.com/decdn/decdn](https://github.com/decdn/decdn): the node, the client, the contracts, and more than two dozen design records covering the payments, the slashing and the verification.

## What testnet means

This is a testnet, so treat it as proof of life, not production. The money is testnet money, the catalogue is what we seeded, and things will break. When they do, tell us: open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/decdn/decdn), find us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/vVbNswKVGX), or write to [info@decdn.org](mailto:info@decdn.org).
